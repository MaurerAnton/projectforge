#160: LiquidityEntryListPage.java

Wicket list page. Extends AbstractListPage. Columns: expectedDateOfPayment, dateOfPayment (with overdue highlighting in red), subject, amount (right-aligned, color-coded green=credit/red=debit), type, paid (checkmark icon), business partner. Sortable. Includes a chart panel at the top — embeds LiquidityChartBuilder output as a JFreeChart PNG image, with settings controls (next days, show/hide paid entries, chart type bar/line).
