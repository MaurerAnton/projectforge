#1643: AddressTextParser.kt

Utility, projectforge-business/src/main/kotlin/org/projectforge/business/address/AddressTextParser.kt
Kotlin object for parsing address information from free text (e.g., email signatures). Uses regex patterns to extract company names (with suffixes like GmbH, AG, Ltd.), email addresses, websites (with known TLDs), phone numbers (with label prefixes and bare format), and ZIP+city combinations. Features PersonNameParser for titles and forms of address.
