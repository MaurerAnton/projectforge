#169: LiquidityFilter.kt

Lines: 69 · Type: Kotlin — extends BaseSearchFilter

Search filter for liquidity entries. Extends the framework's BaseSearchFilter with additional criteria: baseDate (LocalDate — filters entries with payment date on or after this date), paymentStatus (PAID/UNPAID — from PaymentStatus enum in fibu module), amountType (CREDIT/DEBIT). Used by LiquidityForecastBuilder to fetch the right subset of entries for the forecast — in forecast mode queries only UNPAID entries, in historical mode queries all.
