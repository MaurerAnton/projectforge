#171: LiquidityForecast.kt

Lines: 429 · Author: Kai Reinhard · Type: Kotlin Serializable — central forecast model

The beating heart of liquidity planning. 429 lines. A Serializable class (NOT a Spring bean — instantiated manually by LiquidityForecastBuilder) that aggregates three data sources into a unified, sorted list of LiquidityEntry objects. Implements the weighted average payment prediction algorithm.

Three entry sources: liquiEntries (manual entries from LiquidityEntryDO), invoicesLiquidityEntries (from RechnungDO — customer invoices), creditorInvoicesLiquidityEntries (from EingangsrechnungDO — supplier invoices). All merged by build() into a single sorted list.

Payment prediction algorithm (lines 146-347):

  • Historical analysis: calculateExpectedTimeOfPayments() iterates already-paid invoices and records days between invoice date and payment date, grouped by project ID, customer ID, account number, and customer text (both full and first-5-chars). Uses IntAggregatedValues for weighted averages (weighted by invoice amount).
  • Prediction: setExpectedTimeOfPayment() tries each grouping in priority order: project → customer → account → customer text → short text. First match wins. Sets entry.expectedDateOfPayment = invoiceDate + weightedAvgDays.
  • Same algorithm mirrored for creditor invoices (calculateExpectedTimeOfCreditorPayments, grouping by account and creditor text).
  • Probability display: the entry comment shows the match key, area name, weighted average days, and count of paid invoices used for the prediction — e.g. "project#42: ACME Corp: 31 days (15 paid invoices)".

setInvoices / setCreditorInvoices: converts RechnungDO and EingangsrechnungDO collections into LiquidityEntry objects. For paid invoices, uses bezahlDatum (actual payment date); for unpaid, uses faelligkeit (due date). Creditor amounts are negated (.negate() on line 402) because they represent outflows. The ignorePaidStatus flag controls whether historical mode overrides paid status with date-based logic. Type: DEBITOR for customer invoices, CREDITOR for supplier invoices.

Sorting: by dateOfPayment ascending, nulls last, then by subject text.

baseDate: getter returns null if baseDate is today or in the future — used to switch between forecast and historical mode.
