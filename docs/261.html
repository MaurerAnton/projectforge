#261: SkillMatrixPrivacyProtectionJob.kt

Scheduled job for GDPR compliance. Queries users marked as deleted whose retention period has expired, then executes DELETE_ALL_OF_USER to remove all their skill entries. Uses UserDao.getDeletedUsers() to find candidates. Registered in SkillMatrixPlugin. Runs on a cron schedule. Logs each deletion for audit trail.
