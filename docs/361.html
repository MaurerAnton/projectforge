#361: SimplePropertyDelta.java

Hibernate history delta — subtype for simple scalar properties (String, Integer, BigDecimal, Boolean, LocalDate, Enum). The most common delta type — every field edit on an entity creates one. Extends PropertyDelta. Displays in history as "Amount: €1,000.00 → €1,500.00" with property-type-aware formatting (currency, date, percentage, number, text).
