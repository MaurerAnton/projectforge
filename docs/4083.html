Tooltips appear on :hover and :focus (keyboard accessible). Themed properties: dark background with white text for contrast, triangular arrow pointing to the trigger element, 200-300px max-width to prevent overly wide tooltips on long text, fade-in animation with slight vertical offset. Z-index positioned higher than content but lower than modals and notifications. Touch devices get tap-to-show behavior handled by UIkit's JavaScript.
