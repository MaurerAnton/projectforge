#661: DurationUtils.java

Lines: 93 · Author: Kai Reinhard

Duration formatting utility — converts milliseconds to human-readable time strings. 93 lines. Provides getFormattedHoursAndMinutes(millis, withSeconds) → "2:30", "127:15", "2:30:45". Uses TimeUnit.MILLISECONDS.toHours() and .toMinutes() for conversion. Handles negative durations (prepends "-"). The hours component is not capped at 24 — can display "127:15" for multi-day durations, making it suitable for timesheet total display. Also provides getFormattedHours(millis) returning decimal hours (e.g. "2.50" for 2h30m) — used in timesheet reports where decimal format is preferred.
