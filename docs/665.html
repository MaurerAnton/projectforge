#665: MonthHolder.java

Lines: 174 · Author: Kai Reinhard

Calendar month container — represents a complete month with its weeks. 174 lines. Holds: year (int), month (java.time.Month enum), weeks (list of WeekHolder — typically 4-6 weeks for the calendar grid). Used by the calendar view rendering engine (both Wicket and React) to build the month-grid layout.

Static MONTH_KEYS[]: Array of 12 i18n key suffixes: "january", "february", ... — the full i18n key becomes "calendar.month.january" etc. Used for month name labels in calendar headers.

Methods: getFirstDay() — the PFDay representing the first day of the month (always day 1). getNumberOfDays() — 28/29/30/31 based on month + leap year. getWeeks() — builds the list of WeekHolder objects spanning the month. The first week may contain days from the previous month (padding to fill Monday-first or Sunday-first grid), and the last week may extend into the next month. getLabel() — returns localized month name (e.g. "January 2025"). Month values are 1-based (January=1) — consistent with java.time.Month but NOT with the old pre-v7 ConfigureHoliday which was 0-based.
