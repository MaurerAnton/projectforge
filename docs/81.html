Design Decisions

  • Quartz Job with Spring injection via AutowiringSpringBeanJobFactory. Must be idempotent — overlapping executions possible under load.
  • Access control separated from business logic. Authorization checks happen before DAO operations, keeping security centralized.

Gotchas & Pitfalls

  • Jobs are not Spring beans by default. @Autowired injection requires AutowiringSpringBeanJobFactory in the quartz scheduler context.
