#999: RechnungStatus.kt

Enum, projectforge-business/src/main/kotlin/org/projectforge/business/fibu/RechnungStatus.kt
I18n-backed enum defining the lifecycle states of an outgoing invoice: GEPLANT (planned), GESTELLT (issued), ZAHLUNGSERINNERUNG1 (1st reminder), ZAHLUNGSERINNERUNG2 (2nd reminder), GEMAHNT (dunned), STORNIERT (canceled), BEZAHLT (paid). Each status maps to an i18n key under fibu.rechnung.status.*. Provides isIn() for checking inclusion and safeValueOf() for null-safe lookup. Transition from GEPLANT to another status triggers invoice number assignment and date stamping in RechnungDao.
